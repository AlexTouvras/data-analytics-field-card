Where judgement stays

Definitions travel. Reports compose. A person still stops a bad publish.

  1. Define the question. A chart is not a decision. Name what someone will do with the number before a tool is in the room.
  2. Make the grain explicit. Account, customer, day, or claim — named once. A report may compose that unit. It may not remix it.
  3. Reconcile before release. If two packs disagree and nobody owns the gap, the number stays dark.
